HIS STORY

Brahmanandam Kanneganti is a telugu film actor.


He's a Guinness World Record holder for acting in the most number of films by a living actor — over 1,000 films, mostly in Telugu cinema. Known for his timing, expressions, and the way he can steal the scene with just one look or word.
